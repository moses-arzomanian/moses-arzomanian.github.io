Learning design rooted in real leadership

The gap between "I explained it" and "they understood it" is where most training fails, and most designers stop looking.

I've spent thirteen years building training systems for organizations where instruction isn't theoretical; it's the difference between a volunteer who shows up prepared and one who doesn't. As Learning & Development Lead and later Executive Chairperson of the Armenian Youth Federation Canada, I designed onboarding programs, facilitated leadership camps, and managed 250+ volunteers across multiple chapters.

My experience spans both ends of the learning spectrum: from designing youth programs for children and teenagers to facilitating professional development sessions for adult learners in organizational and community leadership contexts. I've delivered training in Canada, Armenia, and internationally, across languages and cultural contexts that most L&D practitioners don't navigate in a career.

My approach starts with the problem, not the content. Before touching a slide, I ask what "understood" looks like at the end of a session, where the highest-risk gaps are, and what the simplest structure is that moves people from confusion to capability.

I hold a Bachelor of Education from Concordia University and certifications in AI evaluation, global diplomacy, organizational change management, and leadership development. I speak English, French, Armenian, and Arabic fluently.

Navio: AI Parenting Coach Platform
Co-founded and designed an AI-powered parenting platform that helps parents navigate behavioural and emotional challenges in children aged 8–17. Built on a Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) architecture fed by 150+ expert guides developed in collaboration with psychologists: every response the AI coach gives is grounded in validated, specialist content, not generic advice. Parents are onboarded through a personalized journey track tailored to their child's specific challenges; when a situation exceeds what the platform can address, parents are referred directly to the partner psychologists. No templated content; every journey is built around the individual child.

Because raising them right starts with knowing how.

Navio is a parenting platform designed to help parents of children aged 8 to 17 teach values, life skills, and problem-solving in a structured, practical way. Through a private parent dashboard, families access curated video and AI-powered personalized content, tailored to the child's specific age, challenges, and what matters most to the family right now.

The platform grows with the child: 151+ clinically validated guides spanning pre-adolescence through late teenage years, with personalized coaching available day or night. The same design philosophy that drives my L&D work: meet the learner exactly where they are.
